# OpenTabletArtist documentation

OpenTabletArtist (OTA) helps you set up and use your drawing tablet with
OpenTabletDriver. Start with [About OTA](OVERVIEW.md) to learn who it's for.

## Get started

- [Install on Windows](INSTALL.md) — download OTA and set up your tablet.

## Use your tablet

- [Home](HOME.md) — your tablets and anything that needs attention.
- [Tablet and mapping](TABLET.md) — choose your display and active area, calibrate, and configure tablet controls.
- [Pen and pressure](PEN.md) — pen input, pressure, and smoothing.
- [Scribble](SCRIBBLE.md) — test your pen and its settings.
- [Settings](SETTINGS.md) — preferences, hotkeys, themes, and drivers.
- [Presets](PRESETS.md) — save and load your tablet settings.
- [Plugins](PLUGINS.md) — understand the plugins OTA uses.
- [Advanced controls](ADVANCED.md) — daemon controls, logs, and diagnostics.
