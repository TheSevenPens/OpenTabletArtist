## What's new

**Each pen button now has three clear choices: Default action, Keyboard shortcut, or
Disabled.** The Pen page has a new buttons tab, and every barrel button on it shows what it
does now with a **…** menu to change it. Default action is the pen's own button; in Windows
Ink mode that is the Barrel Switch (right-click / context menu), and your drawing software
decides what it does through its own stylus settings. A keyboard shortcut is held down for
as long as you hold the pen button. Disabled makes the button do nothing. When two buttons
are both the Barrel Switch, their cards say they do the same thing.

**Record a shortcut by pressing it.** The shortcut editor has a Record button: press the
shortcut on your keyboard and it fills in. A modifier on its own, such as Alt for the
eyedropper, can now be saved too, for pen buttons, ExpressKeys and wheel buttons alike.

**The Pen page is three tabs: basics, pressure and buttons.** Basics keeps the tip and
eraser, how the pen moves and presses, and the pen-input switches. Pressure is as it was.
Buttons holds the barrel buttons and the pen diagram, and only appears for a pen that has
barrel buttons.

**A mouse click or scroll on a button is flagged in Windows Ink mode.** If a pen button,
ExpressKey or wheel button is set to a mouse click or scroll, it now says why that does not
suit Windows Ink: a click can interrupt your stroke, and scrolling does not work. Nothing is
changed for you. A right click on a pen button gets a one-click **Switch to Barrel Switch**,
and the ExpressKey and wheel editor no longer offers its Mouse and Scroll tabs in Windows Ink
mode. This also catches a button set in OpenTabletDriver's own window.

**Pressure and tilt switches grey out when the pen presses like a mouse.** That output takes
neither, so the **Disable pressure sensitivity** and **Disable tilt** switches did nothing
while looking live. They are greyed out with the reason beside them, your choices are kept,
and the Pressure tab says its curve has no effect in that state.

## Fixed

- **The "Unsaved OTD daemon settings" dialog no longer appears after a driver restart when
  you changed nothing.** The driver adjusts your settings as it loads them, and the app was
  comparing its copy against the raw file, so every restart, including the one that follows
  a Pen Dynamics plugin update at launch, looked like unsaved edits.
- **The reconnect and refresh notices beside Save can be dismissed,** and go by themselves
  once they are out of date: after an edit, a save, a reload or a new connection.
- **A tip with nothing bound reads "Disabled"** instead of "Changed to a custom action", and
  offers no Fix; the **Disable pen tip** switch beside it is the way back.

## Also

The Windows Ink notice and its Fix button now sit under the Pressing choice instead of
under Movement, since pressure and tilt are what it is about.

Good to know: a keyboard shortcut on a pen button is held down while the button is held. If
the profile changes or the tablet disconnects at that moment, the key can stay down; press it
once on your keyboard to release it. Windows also does not let a normal program send keys to
an app running as administrator, so a shortcut that works everywhere except one app is worth
checking for that. Troubleshooting has both.

Work continues on macOS and Linux, but only the Windows build is released and supported.
