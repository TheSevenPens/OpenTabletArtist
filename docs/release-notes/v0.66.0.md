## What's new

- **"Quit and stop the daemon" in the tray menu,** for when you want the tablet back to
  its default behaviour on the way out.
- **Connecting to an OpenTabletDriver you installed yourself is now a supported setup,**
  described as such rather than reported as something unexpected.
- **The pressure preview is driven by the driver's own stream,** so it shows what the
  driver sees rather than an approximation.
