## What's new

**The bundled OpenTabletDriver is 0.6.8.1, and it needs the .NET 10 runtime.** The driver that
comes with the app moves up from 0.6.7, and where the old one needed .NET 8 this one needs
.NET 10. If .NET 10 isn't installed, the driver page says so and has a button that takes you to
Microsoft's download. 0.6.8.1 also fixes a Windows crash that some filters triggered.

**The Pen page is plainer.** The basics tab now lays out the tip, movement and pressing, and the
eraser in three columns. The tip and the eraser each offer **Default action** or **Disabled**, in
place of the "Working normally" card and the separate "Disable pen tip" switch. Pressure and tilt
each offer **Enabled** or **Disabled** too: pressure's choice sits on the pressure tab, and tilt
has a tab of its own. Choosing **Default action** puts back the standard binding; it doesn't bring
back a custom one you had before.

**The tilt tab shows your pen's tilt as you move it.** One picture looks down on the tablet and
shows which way the pen leans; the other looks from the side and shows how far over it is. The
side view always draws the pen leaning right, so it doesn't jump from one side to the other as the
lean direction changes.

**Tilt numbers now agree everywhere.** The Test page, the Diagnostics page and the tilt tab read
the same azimuth and altitude for the same pen position. The Test and Diagnostics numbers read
differently from earlier versions because of this.

**The pressure tab opens at the top**, where it used to open scrolled down to the drawing area.

**A change to your displays is picked up.** If you changed monitors while away from the tablet
page, it no longer shows the old layout when you come back.

**The pen picture on the buttons tab** has a rounder eraser end and a longer nib, and no longer
labels the tip and eraser end.
