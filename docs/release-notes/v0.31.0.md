## What's new

- **The calibration overlay is much clearer:** numbered targets, an X-of-Y progress
  count, and pending targets dimmed so you can see where you are.
- **Export and import a calibration capture,** so a good calibration survives a reinstall
  and can be sent to someone else.
- **Pen tilt is captured** alongside position.
- **Duplicate a preset** from the presets list.

## Fixed

- **The cursor stays hidden through the whole calibration hold** instead of flickering
  back.
- **Windows' press-and-hold gesture is disabled on the calibration overlay,** where it
  was interrupting captures.
- **Windows' pen and touch tap-feedback rings are suppressed app-wide** — they were
  drawing over the thing you were trying to aim at.
- **Four-point calibration fits properly.** It used an exact solution that would contort
  the mapping to hit all four points including your aiming error; it now fits the best
  match across them.
