## What's new

**A Deadzone slider on the pressure tab.** The deadzone, how hard the pen must press before it
registers, used to be set only by dragging the pink node at the lower left of the pressure curve
sideways. The new **Deadzone** slider under Softness sets the same thing: move one and the other
follows. The separate "deadzone" number above the live readouts is gone, since the slider shows it.

**Leaving the Pen page no longer undoes a firm Softness.** If you saved a negative Softness (a
firmer curve) and then went to another page, the curve quietly went back to linear. The Scribble
page lost its "Pressure curve" chip and the window said "Unsaved changes" straight after you had
saved. Your saved setting was never lost, but it was no longer the one in use.

**The Pen page's basics tab puts the eraser beside the tip.** It now reads tip, eraser, then
movement and pressing. The tip and eraser choices (**Default action** or **Disabled**) and the
pressure and tilt choices (**Enabled** or **Disabled**) no longer have a line of explanation under
them, since the choice names say it. A tip or eraser that was set up somewhere else still shows
what it does now, and says it may not work as expected.

## Also
