## A new interface

The biggest change since the app started — 59 commits, and the shape of the whole app is
different.

**The sidebar is gone.** Navigation is a horizontal bar of wordmarks across the top, with
tabs (pivots) underneath for the sections within a page. Everything is squared off, and
moving between pages transitions rather than snapping.

**The tablet page went from twelve tabs to five.** Related things are together now:

- **MAPPING** — display mapping, active area and calibration, which are three views of
  one decision
- **DYNAMICS** — pressure, position and tilt
- **CONTROLS** — buttons and wheels
- **PEN** — its own top-level page, holding movement, inputs and buttons
- **About** folded into Home as a two-column layout

**Empty states are heroes** rather than blank panels — when no tablet is connected, the
page says so properly.

**Forget a tablet from its Home card,** with a confirmation, instead of hunting for the
action on a toolbar.

## Fixed

- **The tablet switcher no longer blanks its own selection** during a page transition,
  which made it briefly look as though no tablet was selected.
- **The navigation stops reflowing when you select something.**
