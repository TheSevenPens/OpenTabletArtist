## The interface redesign

This is the big one — 85 commits, and most of them change something you can see.

**The whole app got flatter and quieter.** Boxes and cards gave way to hairline rows,
headings sit on a consistent scale, row actions moved behind a "…" menu instead of
crowding every row with buttons, and the loudest thing on a page is no longer the
delete button.

**The tablet switcher moved into the top bar,** so switching tablets works the same way
from anywhere instead of living in a pane that changed nothing.

**Theming got a proper editor.** Glow editing folded into Appearance with subtabs, a
list and inspector instead of a single control, and linear gradients on all four edges.
Dark Sakura got its generated backdrop, so its bottom glow is finally reachable. Sliders,
combo boxes and the last grey Fluent fills are themed — no more Windows-blue highlights
in a dark skin.

**Settings is reorganised.** Both drivers on one page in two columns, Driver Cleanup as
its own tab, Developer split into six subtabs, and shorter tab names throughout.

**Hotkeys are one list.** Every shortcut in one place, mappings whose preset is gone are
reported instead of silently doing nothing, orphans are cleaned up, and a chord reads as
`Ctrl+Alt+9` rather than `Ctrl+Alt+D9`.

**Calibration leads with its state** and shows what it corrected at each point.

**Buttons and wheels** gained an About column, wheels gained a Clear on each row, and
both lost headings that repeated the tab name.

## Fixed

- **Search sweeps every tab and subtab** — including ones that were hidden — and puts
  you back where you were.
- **OTA asks before stopping a driver it didn't start,** and stops the driver it is
  actually connected to rather than everything sharing that name.
- **Home says "nothing needs attention"** instead of showing a blank column.
- **Fixed-width columns wrap instead of clipping.**
- **Picking a remembered tablet no longer snaps back.**
- **The preset list refreshes when you open its tab,** not just the page.
- **The cursor behaves during a calibration hold.**
- **Menu selection no longer relies on colour alone** to show which item is current.
