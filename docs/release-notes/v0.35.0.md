## What's new

- **Pen Inputs is split, and Pen Buttons gets its own tab.** Tilt Dynamics joins Pressure
  and Position.
- **Turn off pressure or tilt** for a pen, with a note explaining what that means.
- **Pen switch cards are simpler:** either they adapt, or you fix them to one thing.
- **The calibration report opens in a dialog,** so the tab isn't buried under it.

## Fixed

- **The tablet header and its tab rail stay put while the content scrolls,** instead of
  scrolling away and leaving you nowhere to click.
- **Dialog buttons are in Windows order.**
- Tab headings are consistent, and the daemon labels say "bundled" accurately.
